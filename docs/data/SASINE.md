# Land not yet on the Land Register

About 40% of Scotland's land mass was not on the Land Register at the end of February 2026. RoS reported the Land Register at 59.8% of land mass, casework in progress at 4.6% and Indicative Sasines at 31.8% (RoS quarterly progress letter to the Scottish Parliament Economy and Fair Work Committee, 24 Mar 2026). The share held only under the older General Register of Sasines is not published as a single figure. The figure for any month after February 2026 is unknown to us.

## Why it matters here
- RoS INSPIRE parcel outlines come from the Land Register cadastral map. Land still under the Sasine register has no outline, so the map can't show a parcel there.
- Most of this land is thought to be large rural holdings that rarely change hands (a secondary summary of RoS's approach; not a RoS figure).

## What the site does
- That land is labelled **"Not yet on the Land Register"**.
- It is valued by land type and location only, never as a parcel. No outline, title number or owner is shown, and nothing is drawn that looks like a holding boundary.
- No per-holding total is produced for it.
- Provenance label: `stand-in:not-yet-on-land-register`.

## What we don't do
- We don't use ScotLIS or Indicative Sasines data. ScotLIS terms allow private or internal use only, and we don't scrape it.
- We don't buy or download any county cadastral file.
