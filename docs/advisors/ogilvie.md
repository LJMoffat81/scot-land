# Ogilvie reading

A reading of William Ogilvie's published essay. Not a living speaker. The essay is public domain, so a short quotation is allowed. Do not claim any modern group speaks for him.

## Question

What does the 1781/82 essay say the community may claim, and what must stay with the person who improves the land? How should an equal share be described to a reader who has never heard the name?
