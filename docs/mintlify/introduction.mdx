Scot-LAND's purpose is to show **Annual Ground Rent** — the rent of land alone, at every place in Scotland — so land reform and land value tax debates rest on shared, honest evidence. It follows Henry George's idea: the value of land and natural resources comes from nature and from the community, not from any one owner.

Live site: [www.scot-land.scot](https://www.scot-land.scot)

<Warning>
Scot-LAND is **experimental**. It is **not** an official valuation, a rates bill or a tax assessment. **£ figures are not published yet** while calibration is checked. When they are, each figure will carry a confidence label (market, proxy or shadow). For now, figures are published at Data Zone level only, not for individual parcels, plots or addresses.
</Warning>

## What you can use today

- **Find a place**: search by postcode, place name or coordinates, or click the map. You get the Data Zone, settlement and locality for that spot.
- **Open map layers**: official open datasets (mostly Open Government Licence v3.0), such as flood extents, protected sites, woodland, renewable energy sites and community ownership. See the [Map Layer Guide](/map-layer-guide).
- **Public API**: the same data as JSON under `https://www.scot-land.scot/backend`. See the [API Reference](/api-reference).

## Confidence labels

Every Annual Ground Rent figure the engine will publish carries one of three confidence labels. For now, figures are published at Data Zone level only, not for individual parcels, plots or addresses.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **market** | Observed rents from a public source |
| **proxy** | Modelled from open evidence where a direct rent is not available |
| **shadow** | No market price; estimated value of services or sinks that are not traded |

## Commons inputs

The map layers and ledger feed three separate commons classes. They are inputs to Annual Ground Rent and national wealth accounting — not a substitute for the rent figure itself:

- **Surface** (location under homes and businesses)
- **Resource** (extractive)
- **Eco** (natural capital sinks and services)

## Quality tags

Every input the engine holds carries a data-state tag:

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **live** | Observed from a public source as-is |
| **derived** | Computed from open inputs by a stated method |
| **modelled** | Estimated by the residual / mass model (£ figures unpublished until calibration is checked) |
| **placeholder** | Not ready — never served on the public API or map |

## Honesty rules

- Every layer names its source, licence and vintage.
- Missing data is shown as a gap (HTTP `503` "not loaded"). It is never filled with placeholders.
- Constraint overlays such as SSSIs are context. They change best permitted use only; they are not a commons row.
