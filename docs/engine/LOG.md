## 2 October 2026 — Geographically weighted regression

**Decision:** Do not replace the land engine with a geographically weighted regression of infrastructure, demographics, commerce, and environment. Keep stripping the building out of the price, which the signed residual already does. A geographically weighted regression may later be used only to see how those stripped land residuals vary from place to place, and only after a licensed Registers of Scotland sales extract exists. The weights are not assigned by hand. The public pounds stay unchanged.

**Reason:** A full charge on the economic rent of land drives the selling price of the land toward zero. It does not drive the rent to zero. The public still collects the rent. The algorithm must therefore estimate the rent, not read a selling price that the charge itself has removed. Stripping buildings and other human improvements is how that rent is isolated. That is the residual already in the signed path: price, minus the depreciated building, then the speculative cut, then the 5 percent yield, then the land area. Geographically weighted regression is a way to fit location weights to many such residuals. It is not itself the rent. The equation needs a measured land rent at many places before any weight is real. Scotland has no open sale-by-sale file, so those weights cannot be fitted. Inventing them from transit, footfall, schools, turnover, zoning, or sunlight would be a score, and an economist would reject it. Transaction velocity and a zoning premium put speculation and hope back into the base. The signed rule takes speculation out and excludes hope. Local revenues can become a tax on trade. Commute times, footfall, and digital speeds are mostly private feeds. School catchments and stations are location facts only after a coefficient has been fitted to stripped land residuals. They are not added as a list.

**Source:** A note brought to the project on 2 October 2026 proposing an annual rental value for each square metre as a base plus weighted local variables, under a geographically weighted regression. Read against `docs/ENGINE.md`, `docs/DECISION.md`, and the signed residual in `data/config/agr.yaml`. No file in the config was edited.

**Rejected:** Hand-set weights. Real-time feeds. Using a post-charge selling price as the input. Adding transaction velocity, zoning permissions, local revenues, footfall, or solar panels as if they were the rent. Scraping a portal to fill the variables. Replacing Glasgow's near £8,001 or Wick's near £8.28.

**Would fail if:** The weights were fitted to licensed, building-stripped land residuals, the same two public anchors still held, hope and speculation stayed out, and the fit were published with its error. Until that test is passed, this method stays a note.

## 2 October 2026 — GERS and the Group's rule

## 2 October 2026 — The floor of the charge

**Decision:** A payment that makes the land charge negative, meant to draw people to remote places, is rejected. The charge stays the yearly rent of the parcel. At the margin that rent is zero. It does not become a payment. People in a remote place gain because their charge falls with the surplus, taxes on wages, trade, and buildings come off, and one person's share of the national pool is the same as in a city. The existing research credit of £500 a year per person in the Western Isles, Orkney, Shetland, Highland, and Argyll and Bute stays a per-person policy knob. It does not scale with hectares. A settlement grant is spending out of the pool and is not the parcel rent. The public pounds are unchanged.

**Reason:** Ricardo's law says the rent is the surplus over the least productive land in use, and land at that margin pays nothing. Adam Smith treated a bounty that pulls labour onto poorer ground as a distortion. Henry George's invitation to the margin is that the settler keeps what their work produces, because there is no rent to collect. A cheque attached to the land is bid into the price, so the seller takes the encouragement. Paid per hectare, the largest holding takes the largest cheque, and the map stops being a measure of ground rent.

**Source:** `marginal_land_rent` is 0 in `data/config/agr.yaml`. The remote-credit note in that file calls the £500 a research policy knob, not a statute, and lists Na h-Eileanan Siar, Orkney Islands, Shetland Islands, Highland, and Argyll and Bute. `backend/agr/fiscal_year.py` already states that land at the margin pays nothing.

**Rejected:** A negative land assessment. Turning the remote credit into a per-hectare payment. Editing `data/config/agr.yaml`. Moving Glasgow or Wick. Putting a negative charge on the place card.

**Would fail if:** A parcel rent were stored below zero, the margin were given a payment, or the £500 credit were scaled by land area.
