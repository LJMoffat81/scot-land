Scot-LAND is an **experimental**, non-commercial research map of Scotland's commons. It follows Henry George's idea: the value of land and natural resources comes from nature and from the community, not from any one owner. Scot-LAND sets out to show where that value sits.

Live site: [www.scot-land.scot](https://www.scot-land.scot)

<Warning>
Scot-LAND is **experimental**. It is **not** an official valuation, a rates bill or a tax assessment. **No modelled money figures for the commons engine are published yet.** The model is still being tested internally, and its figures stay blocked until they pass review.
</Warning>

## What you can use today

- **Find a place**: search by postcode, place name or coordinates, or click the map. You get the Data Zone, settlement and locality for that spot.
- **Open map layers**: official open datasets (mostly Open Government Licence v3.0), such as flood extents, protected sites, woodland, renewable energy sites and community ownership. See the [Map Layer Guide](/map-layer-guide).
- **Public API**: the same data as JSON under `https://www.scot-land.scot/backend`. See the [API Reference](/api-reference).

## Layers, not one number

The commons are split into three classes: **Surface** (location under homes and businesses), **Resource** (extractive) and **Eco** (natural capital). Each figure is also tagged as *market*, *proxy* or *shadow* value, so different kinds of evidence aren't added together or counted twice.

## Quality tags

Every engine figure carries a data-state tag:

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **live** | Observed from a public source as-is |
| **derived** | Computed from open inputs by a stated method |
| **modelled** | Estimated by the residual / mass model (not published as public money figures until review) |
| **placeholder** | Not ready — never served on the public API or map |

## Honesty rules

- Every layer names its source, licence and vintage.
- Missing data is shown as a gap (HTTP `503` "not loaded"). It is never filled with placeholders.
- Constraint overlays such as SSSIs are context. They change best permitted use only; they are not a commons row.
